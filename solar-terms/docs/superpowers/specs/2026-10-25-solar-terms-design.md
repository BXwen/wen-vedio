# 二十四节气 · 太阳的刻度 — Design

Date: 2026-10-25

## Intent

Recreate and surpass a reference motion-graphics video about the 24 solar terms (二十四节气).
The reference is pure code-generated motion graphics with no voiceover: paper ground, ink wash,
vertical typography, seal red, a 24-term colour wheel, and 24 illustrated cards.

The new piece keeps the reference's proven visual grammar but adds what it lacks: a causal spine
(the sun's position generates every term), seasonal layout variety, real astronomical precision,
and a score that shares one beat grid with the picture.

## Why it works, read from the reference

- **Paper as ground truth.** Every frame sits on warm off-white paper with fibre texture. It reads
  as a printed album, not a screen, which is what makes the 朱红 seals land.
- **Restraint before spectacle.** 6 seconds of near-empty paper before any content. The emptiness
  is the hook.
- **One persistent HUD.** Title top-left, chapter mark top-right, a 24-dot timeline along the
  bottom, citation bottom-right. It never moves, so the viewer always knows where they are in the
  year.
- **Accumulation.** Each season ends with a tally page: a number rising (87 → 180 → 270 → 357)
  and seal badges multiplying. Progress made visible.
- **The wheel is the payoff.** The 24-segment colour ring arrives at the very end as the whole
  system at once.

## What the reference gets wrong (our openings)

1. **No explanation of why 24.** The wheel is decoration. We make it the engine: the sun cursor
   advances continuously and every card is a 15° step.
2. **One template for 24 cards.** 95 seconds of identical layout. We give each season its own
   spatial logic.
3. **Vague dates.** "二月四日前后". We print exact 北京时间 to the minute from DE421.
4. **候 text too small to read.** We set it at ≥28px and give it its own reveal.
5. **Hard cuts between seasons.** We use continuous deformation.
6. **Picture and sound are separate pipelines.** Ours share one JSON beat grid.

## Structure (160 s, 4800 frames @ 30fps)

| Frames | Scene | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 0–240 | Intro | Paper. A vertical stroke. Ink blooms from centre into a landmass silhouette. Title 《二十四节气》 rises vertically. Seal stamps. |
| 240–600 | Numbers | 24 个节气 / 每 15° / 365.2422 天 / 72 候 land one at a time; the ring grows on the right. |
| 600–900 | Seasons | Four colour bands (春夏秋冬) close into the full ring, which then persists as HUD. |
| 900–3900 | Terms | 24 cards × 125 frames. |
| 3900–4800 | Outro | Cursor completes 360°, all segments light, ring collapses into a single seal. |

## Season card layouts

- **春 (立春→谷雨)** — growth. Term name rises from below; painting unfurls upward like a scroll
  being opened; accent green; light comes from above.
- **夏 (立夏→大暑)** — fullness. Painting fills more of the frame; highest key; saturation peaks;
  term name sits centred and large.
- **秋 (立秋→霜降)** — drift. Elements translate laterally; leaves fall; palette desaturates
  progressively across the six cards.
- **冬 (立冬→大寒)** — contraction. Painting recedes and darkens; term name drops low and gains
  weight; border tightens.

## Per-card content

- 节气名 (vertical, large)
- 太阳黄经 (e.g. 315°)
- 精确交节时刻 (2026-02-04 04:02 北京时间)
- 三候 ×3 (readable)
- one 物候 sentence
- the painting, credited
- ring cursor position

## Data

- `data/terms-2026.json` — 24 records from JPL DE421 via skyfield: name, ecliptic longitude,
  UTC instant, Beijing time. Generated by `scripts/gen-terms.py`.
- 24 paintings from the Metropolitan Museum of Art, all `isPublicDomain: true`, Chinese department
  (departmentId 6), hanging scrolls / album leaves / fans.

## Non-goals

- No voiceover. The reference has none; narration would fight the music.
- No image or video generation models. Everything is code, public-domain scans, or synthesis.
- No 4K. 1080p30 matches the reference and renders fast enough to iterate.

## Anti-goals

- A frame-for-frame clone. If the result is indistinguishable from the reference, it failed.
- Decorative animation with no informational job.
- Text the viewer cannot read at 1080p on a phone.

## Constraints

- Windows, Node 24, Remotion 4.0.538.
- Fonts must be local files (no network at render time): NotoSerifSC-VF + SimKai.
- All procedural texture must be deterministic — no `Math.random()`, no CSS animations.
